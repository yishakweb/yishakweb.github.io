About the project

We built the study blog we wished existed in first year.

Study Smarter started as a coursework web development project and grew into a small reference library of techniques that are actually backed by learning-science research, written in plain language.

Purpose

Why this site exists

Most study advice students encounter is passed down informally — highlight the textbook, re-read your notes, pull an all-nighter before the exam. Some of it works a little; most of it wastes time relative to a handful of well-studied alternatives.

Study Smarter exists to close that gap: to take findings from cognitive and educational psychology and turn them into short, specific, usable guides, rather than another motivational listicle.

Audience

Who it's for

The primary audience is secondary school and university students preparing for continuous assessments and final exams, particularly those juggling multiple subjects with limited revision time.

Educators and self-directed learners outside formal education make up a secondary audience — the underlying techniques apply to any subject that requires retaining and applying information over time.

Scope

What's in and what's out

In scope

Technique guides on memory, planning, active recall and study wellbeing; a searchable, filterable article index; a contact channel for reader questions; and simple account creation so a future version can save reading progress.

Out of scope (for now)

Subject-specific tutoring content, a real payment or subscription system, and server-side storage — this build uses the browser's local storage to simulate accounts and messages for demonstration purposes.

Problem statement

The gap between effort and results

Many students report spending long hours studying without a proportional improvement in results. Left unguided, students tend to default to the study methods that feel most productive in the moment — re-reading, highlighting, passive review — rather than the methods that produce durable learning, such as spaced retrieval practice. This site addresses that gap directly, one technique at a time.
